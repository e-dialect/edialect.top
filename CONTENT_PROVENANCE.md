A future, separately reviewed provenance or rewrite project may investigate
sources, obtain permission, add attribution, or propose replacement text. Such
work must be treated as a product-content change rather than folded into a
software-license housekeeping PR.

Future content additions must record the source, access or revision date,
applicable license or permission, and any required attribution before
